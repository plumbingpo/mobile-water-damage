A sump pump that quits, a sewer that backs up, a pipe that lets go, rising river water, storm surge or a hard rain can each flood a property, and the damage should be handled by professionals without delay. Expect gray water (Category 2) or black water (Category 3) in almost any flood, and with it bacteria, contamination and potentially hazardous substances that threaten your health. Day or night, 24/7, Mobile Restoration Co sends flood cleanup crews throughout Mobile to remove floodwater, sanitize the affected rooms and safely restore your home or building.

We start with fast water extraction, using truck-mounted and submersible pumps that can move thousands of gallons per hour. Next our technicians, who hold IICRC certification, judge the level of contamination and choose a sanitizing plan that covers HEPA air filtration, antimicrobial application and disinfecting each surface that got wet. We tear out and properly dispose of what cannot be saved, such as soaked carpet padding, contaminated drywall and ruined insulation.

Once the space is extracted and sanitized, our team brings in commercial drying gear, namely low-grain refrigerant (LGR) dehumidifiers plus high-velocity air movers, so the structural components dry all the way through. We check moisture levels every day with calibrated meters and thermal imaging so the drying is complete and mold does not get a start. From start to finish we document each step for your insurance claim, with the moisture maps, equipment logs, photos and scope-of-work reports adjusters ask for.

Why Hire a Professional for Flood Cleanup?

There is much more to a flood cleanup than removing the water you can see. The water brings sewage, chemicals, bacteria and more with it, and all of that is a serious hazard to health. Professional flood crews are trained in containment, personal protective equipment (PPE) and sanitizing protocols that keep your family from exposure to pathogens such as E. coli, hepatitis and other waterborne diseases.

Health is not the only issue: when cleanup is done wrong, moisture stays hidden and heavy mold can follow in a matter of days. With thermal imaging, psychrometric calculations and moisture meters, trained technicians confirm that subfloors, structural framing, wall cavities and every other wet area dry to an acceptable standard. Nothing else reliably heads off secondary damage, and that damage can run thousands more to remediate, so we dry by the numbers.

Flood Cleanup Prevention Tips

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • Wherever flooding is likely, get appliances and electrical equipment up above floor level
  • Seal foundation cracks and apply a waterproofing coating to basement walls
  • Clear leaves and debris from storm drains and gutters, which lets water drain the way it should
  • Store valuables and important papers off the basement floor in waterproof containers
  • Add a sump pump with battery backup, since power outages and storms tend to arrive together
  • Add downspout extensions so roof water empties well away from the foundation

When to Call for Flood Cleanup in Mobile

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • Basement flooding after the sump pump quit in a downpour
  • Soaked landscaping that leaves water pooling against your foundation
  • Receding water that leaves muddy residue and debris behind
  • Roof or wall openings left by a storm, with rain pouring through
  • Carpet that squishes underfoot or drywall that feels soft and wet
  • Tide marks on drywall or trim where the water peaked
  • Living areas flooded by contaminated water from a backed-up drain or sewer
