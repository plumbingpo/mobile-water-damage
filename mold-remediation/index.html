How to Tell You Need Mold Remediation

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • Closets, bathrooms or other damp, enclosed rooms that always smell musty
  • Furniture against outside walls or items in closets developing speckled patches
  • Long-running hidden leaks, for example at a dishwasher line, sink drain or roof flashing
  • Headaches, allergy flare-ups or breathing symptoms that ease whenever you leave the house
  • A flood or leak in the recent past that never got a professional dry-out
  • Signs of concealed moisture such as paint that bubbles, wallpaper that peels or materials that warp
  • Moisture beading up again and again on glass, walls or anything cold

Why Mold Remediation Is Not a DIY Job

This is not a job to take on yourself. Disturbing mold colonies without proper containment sends millions of spores airborne, which can contaminate unaffected areas of your home and create worse health exposure than the original problem. Our crews work in personal protective equipment inside a negative-pressure enclosure, with HEPA air scrubbers running, to trap spores and hold them in the work area while removal is underway.

Just as important, we deal with moisture, the underlying cause of every mold problem. Clean off the visible growth but leave the moisture problem, and the mold comes back, in many cases in a matter of weeks. Our certified technicians are trained and equipped to track down moisture you cannot see, dry the affected areas the right way and make the corrections that keep mold from recurring.

Mold Remediation Prevention Tips

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly mold issues down the road. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • In bathrooms, basements and other damp areas, choose paint and drywall rated mold-resistant
  • Make sure crawl spaces are properly ventilated and consider installing a vapor barrier
  • Clean and maintain your HVAC system and change filters regularly to prevent mold in ductwork
  • Clear gutters and downspouts regularly, which keeps rain from soaking the foundation walls
  • Fix water leaks within 24 hours, because mold needs only moisture and 24 to 48 hours to start growing
  • Use ventilation and a dehumidifier to hold humidity under 60%, with 30 to 50% as the ideal
