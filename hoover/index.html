The First Hour After Water Damage in Hoover

People mean well in the first hour after a leak, and some of what they do makes things worse. These are the mistakes our crews see most often in Hoover, and what to do instead.

Do not use a household vacuum on water. It is an electric shock risk and it will ruin the machine; use towels and a mop. Leave ceiling fixtures off if the ceiling above is wet, and keep away from any part that droops. Do not lift tacked-down carpet yourself, since it can shrink or tear and may be saveable if it is handled properly. Do not turn the heat up high to dry things out, because warm, damp air is what mold likes.

Do shut off the water, open cabinet doors and closets so air can reach them, and hang up wet fabrics and cushions to drip. Photograph each room before you shift a single item. Do keep children and pets away from the area. And do make the call early, so Mobile Restoration Co can have a crew on the way to Hoover while you do the rest.

Where Water Damage Starts in Hoover Properties

Some water problems need a phone call this minute, and others can safely wait until morning. We have ordered these five common Hoover calls by how fast each one needs attention.

Bursts: Nothing is more time-critical than a broken water line, which keeps pouring until the supply is cut. Cold weather, depending on the season, is one trigger, with the leak appearing as the frozen section thaws. Shut the house supply, run a tap to drain the lines and phone us immediately, whatever the hour. If a ceiling is sagging from the weight of water, stay out from under it and do not poke it.

Runoff: Storm water coming under a door or through a garage is next in line. You cannot stop the rain, but you can move belongings up and keep away from any outlets near the water. This water has crossed lawns, driveways and streets, which makes it unsanitary. As soon as it is safe to enter, we pump the water out and take away porous items it ruined.

Kitchens: A dishwasher that leaks from its door or a refrigerator line that drips behind the unit calls for same-day attention. Turning off the supply valve for that appliance normally ends the leak itself. The harm continues, though, since water has already run under cabinets and flooring where air does not move. For Hoover kitchen jobs, our crew can frequently save the cabinets by pushing dry air in at the toe kick.

Crawlspaces: Moisture under a raised floor is seldom an emergency, but it should not be put off for a season. When we look underneath, we tend to find water lying on the liner, insulation that is soaked or sweating ducts. Left alone, the damp air rises into the rooms above and the wood framing begins to decay. We extract the water, dry the wood to a safe reading and tell you what we think the source is.

Odors: Last on the list is a stale, earthy odor in a room that once had a leak, which is worth a scheduled visit. Mold can begin within 24 to 48 hours of a material getting wet, so a leak from months ago had plenty of time. Do not scrub or spray a large patch yourself, because disturbing it spreads spores through the house. Our technicians test the area, contain it and remove the growth along with the moisture feeding it across Hoover.

How Drying Works on a Hoover Water Damage Job

Homeowners in Hoover often want to know what living with drying equipment is actually like. The first day covers extraction, taking out unsalvageable materials, and placing air movers and dehumidifiers where the water went. The equipment is loud, about like several large fans, and it makes the room warm. It needs to stay on around the clock, and turning it off, shifting it or airing out the room slows everything down.

Each day after that, a technician visits to take moisture readings in the same spots and record them. Equipment is repositioned toward the slower spots as others finish, and surplus units leave the house. The technician shares the readings and explains the trend.

Drying usually lasts 3 to 5 days. Longer jobs happen when the leak went unnoticed, when insulation or hardwood got soaked, or when more than one level is wet. When every reading matches the dry standard, Mobile Restoration Co removes the equipment and gives you the drying record for your Hoover property. Only then should rebuilding start, because closing up damp walls traps the problem inside.
